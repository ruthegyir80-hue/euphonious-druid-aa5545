Shortlist findings

The strongest candidate on the current evidence is Pavago, because it publishes an Africa-wide talent programme and includes Ghana-based profiles. Opportunity profiles

Reviewed companies

Pavago

Talent marketplace

Eligibility: Plausible but unconfirmed for Ghana-based applicants.

  • Open to candidates in Africa according to the company’s Africa page.
  • Roles include Virtual Assistant, Executive Assistant, and Customer Service Representative.
  • Joining is free and no direct fee was found.

Crossover

Customer support & success

Eligibility: Ghana page exists, but no public support roles were open at the last check.

  • Application is via the Ghana support page.
  • Roles are long-term, remote and assessment-heavy.
  • Consider weekly re-checking due to changing openings.

Afri VA Hub

Virtual assistant recruiting

Eligibility: Claims to recruit only from Africa, but business signals require verification.

  • Career page states African recruitment focus.
  • Use of generic testimonials and a Google Form raises caution.
  • Verify before sharing personal documents or credentials.

beehiiv

Customer support

Eligibility: Ghana is explicitly excluded by the employer’s own wording.

  • Aggregator labels were inconsistent with the official job text.
  • Employer wording overrides third-party country tags.
  • Do not rely solely on aggregators for eligibility.

OnlineJobs.ph

Remote work platform

Eligibility: Not eligible for Africa-based workers.

  • Company page explicitly excludes workers from Africa.
  • Not a viable path for Ghana-based applicants.
  • Best treated as a closed route for this brief.

Wing Assistant

Administrative staffing

Eligibility: No Ghana-specific roles found; country-specific postings dominate.

  • US-only roles were more common in search results.
  • Check directly before applying to any role.
  • Not a clear fit from current evidence.

Key findings

What this research shows

1

Official pages win

The employer’s own wording should override aggregator labels, especially where those labels conflict with role requirements.

2

Ghana eligibility is narrow

Almost all of the clearly relevant roles either excluded Ghana or had no confirmable public posting at the time of review.

3

Watch for red flags

Unclear business information, generic testimonials, and requests for personal email applications are strong reasons to pause and verify.

Recommended next steps

Action plan

A

Create a Pavago profile

Set up a profile and monitor the client roles that appear for remote administrative and support work.

B

Check Crossover weekly

The Ghana support page may open new support roles, but it needs regular checks.

C
