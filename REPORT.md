# coRemote Opportunity Research & Vetting Report

Prepared by: Ruth Salvage Egyir | Executive Assistant | Operations Support Specialist
Research date: 15 October 2026
Project type: Research and vetting sample (Virtual Assistant / Administrative / Customer Support)

---

1. The brief

A client needs a shortlist of companies that hire remote Virtual Assistants, Administrative Assistants or Customer Support Assistants and can accept applicants based in Ghana. The brief set three rules: use official company pages first, never invent vacancies or links, and label anything that cannot be verified.

2. Method

- Searched job boards and aggregators for appropriate roles and filters.
- Opened each coRemote Opportunity Research & Vetting Report entry and checked the employer's own careers or application page wherever it was accessible.
- Recorded seven fields per company: company and website, role title, application link, Ghana eligibility and the evidence for it, required skills, remote scope, and fees or warning signs.
- Ranked evidence: employer's own page first, then third-party listings. If the two disagreed, the employer's page won.
- Labelled each finding: Verified, Plausible but unconfirmed, or Unverified.

4. Opportunity profiles

Pavago (talent marketplace)

Website: https://pavago.co

Roles: Virtual Assistant, Executive Assistant, Customer Service Representative, Appointment Setter (hired by client companies)

How to apply: create a candidate profile at https://candidates.pavago.co; companies then send interview requests

Ghana eligibility: Pavago's Africa page invites candidates from anywhere in Africa, and its talent listings include Ghana-based candidates. It does not name Ghana as eligible, so this is plausible but unconfirmed.

Typical requirements: 1-2 years of VA, admin or operations support experience; Google Workspace and Microsoft Office; strong written and spoken English. Some client roles ask for a recorded video intro.

Remote scope: varies by client posting. One posting was tagged worldwide; an earlier one was listed for Pakistan only. Check each role.

Fees and warning signs: Pavago states joining is free and that nobody will ask for money. No red flags found. Apply only through the Pavago site.

Crossover (recruiter for US software companies)

Website: https://crossover.com

Roles: Customer Support and Success

How to apply: https://crossover.com/jobs/customer-support/gh

Ghana eligibility: the page is set up for Ghana, but its snapshot (about 45 days old when checked) said no public support roles were open in the region. Unverified for now: re-check the page.

Typical requirements: long-term, full-time contracts and a multi-stage online assessment process.

Fees and warning signs: no application fee found. Public reviews are mixed: some applicants complain about long assessments with little feedback, and one reviewer reports activity monitoring.

5. Proceed with caution

Afri VA Hub: its career page says it hires only from Africa, but it lists a placeholder-looking US address, uses generic testimonials and takes applications through a Google Form. These are observations, not proof of wrongdoing. Verify the business before sharing documents.

6. Key finding: aggregators can contradict employers

Himalayas tagged beehiiv's Customer Support role as open to candidates from all countries. The posting text itself lists Ghana among the countries it will not accept. Anyone relying on the tag alone would have wasted an application. Rule applied: the employer's own wording overrides the aggregator's label.

7. Limitations

No live, role-specific vacancy was confirmed as open to Ghana-based applicants on the day of research.

Several company sites were unavailable or blocked, and some evidence came from third-party mirrors. Each is labelled above.

Listings change often. Every link should be re-checked before anyone applies.

8. Recommended next steps for the client

- Create a Pavago profile and review the client roles that appear.
- Check the Crossover Ghana page weekly for new support openings.
- Before applying anywhere, confirm eligibility on the employer's own page and never pay a fee.
