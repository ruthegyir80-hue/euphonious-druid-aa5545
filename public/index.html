Below are examples of my work including Notion operations systems, monday.com onboarding boards, Make automations, executive email templates, Slack workspace organization, and professional meeting agenda templates.

Client Onboarding Board — monday.com

A monday.com board that walks each new client through onboarding. Tasks such as collecting client information, scheduling the kickoff meeting, and completing the onboarding review are colour-coded by status (Working on it, Done, Stuck), with due dates, attached files, and a timeline summary so nothing slips through the cracks.

A Make scenario that watches a Notion data source for new items and automatically creates a matching item in monday.com. Scheduled to run every 15 minutes, it keeps both tools in sync without manual copy-and-paste.

A weekly reporting page in Notion where each week is logged as its own entry with the reporting period, the person who created it, and the reporting date. It sits alongside a Client Operations Hub, Calendar Management SOP, and Meetings pages in the same workspace.

Executive Inbox — Email Templates

A library of ready-to-use email templates for the situations an Executive Assistant handles every day — starting with meeting requests that offer three time options. Placeholders in [brackets] make each template quick to personalise before sending.
